Running your life takes nine jobs — and the tools don't talk to each other.

BUDGET APP· BOOKING SITE· BROKER· TRADE LOG· BOOKKEEPING· COMPLIANCE· TAX APP· FP&A· AI WORK JOURNAL

You can buy all nine — that's the problem. Nine logins, nine subscriptions, nine copies of your life that never meet. The heavy ones — compliance, FP&A — are priced for companies, not people. And to get the one answer that matters, you become the integration: copying numbers from app to app into a spreadsheet.

None of them can answer the only question that matters:

How long does my money last?

SAME NINE MODULES · DIFFERENT REASONS

FOUNDER

Building a company? See how many months you've got, what the build is costing, and receipts for every dollar when investors ask. When someone asks where the money went, the answer is one click.

TRADER

Trading your own money? Every fill lands in your books, keeps its receipt, and shows up on your return — you just trade. No April spreadsheet panic; it's been ready all year.

CREATOR

Filming what you do? Your day plans your shoots, and your calendar writes the script. More posting, less planning.

NOMAD

Living out of a suitcase? Book the trip, and the budget, calendar, and books update themselves — from anywhere. Change countries; your money system doesn't care.

SMALL BUSINESS OWNER

Running a small business? Know what came in, what went out, and what's left — without hiring a bookkeeper. You see what an accountant would see, in plain words, every day.

STUDENT

First bank account? Learn where your money goes before it's gone — the app shows you how. You'll pick up real accounting without ever taking the class.
